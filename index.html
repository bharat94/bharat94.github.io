§ 01 — About

I write software the way some people keep journals.

Mostly for myself, sometimes for others, always because the act of building makes the problem smaller. From CLI utilities to full-stack apps, I find the whole stack interesting — no loyalty to any one layer.

the part I actually like →

I'm drawn to systems that you can feel — ones that respond quickly, fail loudly, and explain themselves. I'd rather ship something clean and small than something big and nervous.
